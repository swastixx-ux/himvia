ABOUT HIMVIA

Apple operations
in Rohru, Shimla.

Founder Swastik Sainseth has managed apple grading and packing since 2024, overseeing 25+ seasonal workers, custom cartons and transport. He introduced buyer-branded cartons and hired a manager to oversee daily operations.

Himvia works with 100+ farming families and handles 2,500 tonnes of apples per season. We earn revenue through brokerage commissions, grading and packing services, and carton sales.

We are expanding repeat buyer orders and local broker partnerships. Our growth plans include direct supply, inventory and payment tools, and additional crops and sourcing regions for year-round business.
